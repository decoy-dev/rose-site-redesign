Every component, once.

A reference for building the other pages. Copy the markup from here or from _partials.md. Don't edit site.css.

Motion: every behaviour, what it does and where it runs on this page. Each one plays once as its graphic arrives; reload to see it again. Under reduced motion, or without JavaScript, everything is simply in place.

  • lead: the card drops in, the dot pulses once, the source lines write in, the status steps along. Runs on .lead-card.
  • serp: the query writes into the bar, the results arrive in order, the practice's own result is ringed last. Runs on .serp.
  • ad: the ad fills in, the keyword underlines itself, a ripple lands on the headline. Runs on .ad-card.
  • lp: headline, photo and fields fill in order, and the button presses once. Runs on .lp-thumb.
  • call, as a thread and call, ringing: messages arrive in turn; a ringing call counts three seconds, connects, then shows its own line. Runs on .thread and .call.
  • schedule: open slots sit still and the booked slot slides into its row. Runs on .schedule.
  • report: the three numbers count up, the line draws, bars grow, the lead line and "What we're changing" arrive last. Runs on .report-panel and .report-chart.
  • feed: items arrive one by one, the avatar settles and the italic word writes in. Runs on .feed.
  • cadence: each dot fills as it is reached and the line draws on to the next. Runs on .cadence.
  • moment: the pieces of a stage card arrive one after another, the raised card last and furthest. Runs on .beat-screen > .moment.
  • numbered cards: the numeral counts up to its number. Runs on .commit-num and .step-num.
  • signature: writes in from left to right. Runs on .signature.
  • rise, stagger, count, draw, status: the five hooks a page builder adds by hand.
  • loops: a slow CSS loop on a sample graphic, only while it is on screen: the status chips step from new to called to consult booked. Runs on [data-loop="status"]. Its first frame is the finished graphic; under reduced motion it does not run.
  • signed samples: GSAP loops of about 8 to 10s in three beats (place, mark, rest): the search types itself and this month's page lands, signed by Web and SEO ([data-loop="local-page"]); a consult is traced down the source ledger, signed by Paid media ([data-loop="ledger"]); a band walks to each signed change in the monthly report and its stamp is pressed on ([data-loop="report"], see after you say yes). Only on screen with the tab visible; paused animations jump each one to its finished, signed state; never under reduced motion. Their panels ([data-place]) settle 16px into place once, at full opacity.
  • the comparison: many leads arrive and most fade to a ring; fewer arrive and each is booked. Loops while on screen. Runs on .compare[data-loop="compare"].
  • founders ring: on About the five discs arrive once, in ring order, --stagger-ring apart, then the line at the centre. Runs on .founders-ring[data-ring]; under reduced motion or without JavaScript the ring is simply there. The homepage ring talks instead ([data-ring-talk], see the forest band): its centre crossfades, opacity only, to the founder you point at, focus or tap. Its one-off introduction, when the band is about 40% in view: the discs swing out of the centre along arcs in ring order, each crescent cutting in from the outer edge as it lands, the fact rises into view, then the sage highlight goes once round the ring and settles on the fact (about 2.75s, never loops; on phones each disc rises into its place in the row). Pointing, focusing, tapping or Pause animations end it at once; under reduced motion, with animations paused or without JavaScript the ring is simply there.
  • accordion: the answer opens and closes by height and opacity, and the plus turns with it.
  • buttons and links: a wash blooms from where the pointer came in, the button lifts and its arrow passes through; a text link's arrow nudges.
  • closing panel: the italic word of the tagline settles last.
  • Header: a hairline grows under it once the page has scrolled, and the current page's underline draws in. Scroll this page to see it.
  • Homepage hero, once on load ([data-hero-intro]): the headline rises into place from its first frame, "know" writes in like a signature, the motif window opens ring by ring from the bloom's centre and the sage crescent under the disc edge draws out from 60% (all CSS); on screens wider than 900px the founders' letter is delivered at the same time (GSAP, about 2.9s, see the hero letter). The email capture never moves. Under reduced motion, without JavaScript or with animations paused the hero is complete from the start. Inner page heroes: the italic word of the headline settles into its colour.
