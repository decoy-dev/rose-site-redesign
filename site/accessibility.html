Accessibility

Everyone who runs a practice should be able to read this site, find what we do and get in touch with us. This page says the standard we hold the site to, how we check it, and what we know still falls short.

Last reviewed October 14, 2026.

The standard we work to: the accessibility standard for this website

We build this site to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. Based on our own testing, we believe it does. No outside auditor has checked it yet.

What that means on this site: accessibility features of this website

  • Every page works with a keyboard alone. The first key press shows a link that skips straight to the content, and the item you are on always has a visible outline.
  • Text and buttons meet the AA contrast levels or better. The lightest text on the cream background measures 5.88 to 1, and body text 13.78 to 1.
  • Pages fit a screen 320 pixels wide and still work when you zoom to 200 percent, with nothing cut off and no sideways scrolling.
  • Headings run in order, every form field has a label, and a form error is announced and shown next to the field it belongs to.
  • The sample screens that show how our work looks, such as a search result or a report, each have a written description for screen readers.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, nothing on the site slides, loops or fades in. The text and graphics are simply in place.
  • Anything that moves on its own can be stopped. Use Pause animations at the foot of any page. The site remembers your choice on this device.

How we test it: how we test this website's accessibility

On October 14, 2026 we checked every page of the site, on a desktop-sized screen and a phone-sized screen. We ran the axe automated checker against WCAG 2.2 AA, went through every page with the keyboard only, checked the layout at 320 pixels wide and at 200 percent zoom, and checked the site with reduced motion turned on in Chrome. We check again whenever we change a page's layout.

What we know falls short: known accessibility issues on this website

  • We haven't yet tested the site with a screen reader user. An automated checker finds only some problems, so this is the next test we'll run.
  • Each sample screen is described as one picture, so a screen reader reads a short summary rather than every label on it. The same information is in the text around it.
  • The chat window in the corner of each page comes from an outside service, and we can't change all of how it works. If it gets in your way, the form or email reach the same people.

Tell us if something doesn't work: report an accessibility problem

If any part of this site is hard to use, write to hello@rosepracticesolutions.com or send us a message. Tell us the page and what happened. One of our founders replies within one business day, and if you need something from the site in another format, we'll send it.
