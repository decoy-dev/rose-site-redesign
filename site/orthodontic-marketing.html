Orthodontic marketing questions

How much does orthodontic marketing cost?

At Rose Practice Solutions the Core Marketing Plan is $2,000 a month for one or two locations, $2,500 for three and $3,000 for four or more. Patient follow-up is an add-on, from $500 a month, priced by lead count. Ad spend is separate and paid to Google and Meta. Every number is on the pricing page.

How do orthodontists get more new patients?

A few things have to work together. Families nearby have to find the practice when they search. The page or the ad they land on has to answer what they asked. And when they ask for a consult, somebody has to call them back. We build the first two, judge the work by consults booked, and tell you each month which part is working. If calling back is the gap, patient follow-up is an add-on you can ask us about.

Do you guarantee a number of new patients?

No. We never promise a patient count. What you get is a monthly report that shows how many leads came in, how many we reached, how many booked a consult, and what we're changing next. We judge the work by consults booked, not by how many leads came in.

Is there a long contract?

The first 90 days from launch are a commitment. After that it's month to month, with 30 days' notice. The monthly report keeps coming until the last day.

Who owns the website and the ad accounts?

You do. You own the site, the domain and the content. The Google and Meta ad accounts are in your own name and you pay the platforms directly, so the history stays yours.

We already have a marketing agency. Can you call our leads?

Patient follow-up is a monthly add-on to the Core Marketing Plan. Any client can ask about it, and our sales team confirms the fit.

Do you use AI to make the marketing or call patients?

We use software for production, the way a photographer uses Lightroom. The strategy, the words, the design and every conversation come from a named person. No AI on the phone or in an inbox pretending to be a person, and no generated patients or generated smiles. The rest is in our AI policy.

Do you only work with orthodontists?

No. Most of our work is built around orthodontic practices, and dental practices are welcome. If you're a dentist, the dental marketing page says what that means for you.

Is ad spend included in the price?

No. Ad spend goes to Google and Meta directly, from ad accounts in your name. We suggest a budget on the first conversation, based on your market and how many consults you can take.

What is patient follow-up?

Patient follow-up is an add-on to the Core Marketing Plan. A patient concierge calls and texts your new leads and past leads, within calling hours set for your practice, and books the consult into your own patient management software. The lead might come from an ad, your website or your Google profile. It's from $500 a month, priced by lead count, and our sales team will see whether it fits your practice.

How is Rose Practice Solutions different from other orthodontic marketing agencies?

Across our team we have more than 60 years of combined dental and orthodontic experience, clinical and business side. The people in the proposal are the people who do the work and answer your questions. The work is built from your practice, so if it could run for another practice with a logo swap, it doesn't ship. We judge it by consults booked, not by how many leads came in. And every monthly report says what we're changing next.
