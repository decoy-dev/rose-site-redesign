Patient follow-up, an add-on

Patient follow-up means a patient concierge calls and texts your new and past leads, during calling hours set for your practice, and books the consult into your own patient management software. Any client can ask about adding it, and our sales team decides whether it fits the practice.

From new, to called, to consult booked.

Each lead moves through the same three states, and you can see where every one of them stands. Across our team we have more than 60 years of combined dental and orthodontic experience, clinical and business side, so the people calling your leads know the treatment and the questions patients ask.
