Questions owners ask about follow-up

Can we add follow-up?

Any client can ask about it. Our sales team talks it through with you and decides whether it fits the practice.

Do you call past leads too?

Yes. Follow-up covers new leads and past leads, so the people who asked about treatment months ago hear from you again.

What if the lead doesn't answer?

We try again, within the calling hours set for your practice. A missed call gets a text and another call after that. How long we keep trying is agreed with you at the start, and every attempt is in the report, so you can see how far each lead got.

How do you handle patient information?

The way the law expects. We sign a business associate agreement before we see any of it, calls and texts run through a system set up for health information, and nothing about a patient goes into an ad platform.

Can we see what was said?

Yes. Every call and text is logged against the lead. The monthly report lists the leads, the attempts and the outcomes, including the ones that didn't book.

Does this work with our patient management software?

Usually. Where your software has a web login, the patient concierge books straight into it. Where it doesn't, we set up a secure remote connection with your IT contact before the first lead comes in.
