What owners ask about paid ads

How much do Google Ads cost for an orthodontist?

There are two costs: our part, inside the Core Marketing Plan at $2,000 a month for one or two locations, $2,500 for three and $3,000 for four or more, and the ad spend, which you pay Google and Meta directly. We suggest a budget on the first conversation, based on your market and how many consults you can take. The first 90 days from launch are a commitment. After that it's month to month, with 30 days' notice. The rest is on the pricing page.

Is ad spend included in the price?

No. Ad spend goes to Google and Meta directly, from ad accounts in your name, so the history stays yours.

How do we know which ad brought a patient in?

We set up call and conversion tracking, so you can see which ad led to which call and which call became a consult. The results go in the monthly report, and we judge each ad by the consults it books, not by its clicks.

Who calls the leads the ads bring in?

Your front desk, or us. Patient follow-up is an add-on any client can ask about, and our sales team decides whether it fits the practice. With it, a patient concierge calls and texts your new and past leads and books the consult into your own patient management software.
