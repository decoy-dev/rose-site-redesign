What owners ask about websites and SEO

How much does an orthodontic website cost?

The website is part of the Core Marketing Plan: $2,000 a month for one or two locations, $2,500 for three and $3,000 for four or more. The same plan covers website care, local SEO, your Google Business Profile, and Google and Meta ads management. Ad spend is separate, paid to Google and Meta. The first 90 days from launch are a commitment. After that it's month to month, with 30 days' notice. The rest is on the pricing page.

Can we take the website without the ads?

Yes. A website without the ads is one of our projects, priced for your practice.

Do we own the website?

Yes. You own the site, the domain and the content. They're yours, not ours.

Is the website built from a template?

No. It's built from your practice. You choose from three directions, and we build the one you pick.

Who do we email when something on the site breaks?

A named person. Website care after launch covers updates and fixes, and it's part of the plan.
