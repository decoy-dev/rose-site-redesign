What does the plan include?

The Core Marketing Plan includes a website, local SEO, and Google and Meta ads management, from the founding team. It all ends up in one monthly report, signed by the people who did the work.

  • A new website at onboarding, built from your practice. Three directions to choose from, two rounds of revisions. Our web and SEO lead builds it.
  • Website care after launch: updates, fixes and a named person to email when something breaks.
  • Your Google Business Profile, kept current.
  • Local SEO, so families nearby find you for the treatments you want to do more of.
  • Google Ads aimed at patients who are ready to start treatment, built and optimized weekly by the person who runs your ads.
  • Meta ads, same person, same report.
  • A monthly report that lists what changed, who changed it, what it brought in, and what we're changing next.

Questions about pricing

Why are your prices public?

Because you'd ask, and because a price you have to ask for isn't a price. Most agencies quote after the call. We'd rather you know before it.

Is ad spend included?

No. Ad spend goes to Google and Meta directly, from ad accounts in your name, so the history stays yours. We suggest a budget on the first conversation, based on your market and how many consults you can take.

Can I add patient follow-up?

Any client can ask about it. Patient follow-up is an add-on, from $500 a month, priced by lead count, and our sales team will talk it through with you to see whether it fits your practice.

What happens after 90 days?

You stay month to month. Thirty days' notice ends it, and the monthly report keeps coming until the last day.

What can we buy on its own, outside the plan?

Three projects, each priced for your practice, so there's no number here: brand and logo work, video and content, and a website without the ads. Tell us which one when you get in touch.
