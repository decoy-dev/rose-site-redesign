Privacy policy

Rose Practice Solutions runs this website. This page says what we collect when you visit, why we collect it, and who helps us handle it.

Last updated October 2026.

What we collect: information Rose Practice Solutions collects

  • What you tell us on the contact form: your name, practice name, email, phone number, how many locations you have, and what you'd like help with. If you use the email field on the homepage, we get only your email address.
  • What you tell us in the chat on this site: your name, your phone number and your message, and whether you ticked the box agreeing to texts.
  • How you arrived: the page you landed on, and any campaign tags in the link you clicked, such as which ad it came from.
  • How the site is used: pages viewed, clicks and scrolling, recorded by Microsoft Clarity. Clarity is set to its strictest masking, so the text on the page and anything you type are hidden in what it records.
  • Measurement tags loaded through Google Tag Manager, which may set cookies.

What we use it for: how we use your information

We use your details to call, text or email you about the request you sent. We use the visit data to see which pages and ads bring people to us, and to fix what isn't working on the site. We don't sell your information.

Text messages

If you give us your mobile number in the chat and tick the box agreeing to texts, we may text you about your question, to set up a call, and with news and offers about our services. Some texts may be sent by automated means. How often we text depends on the conversation. Message and data rates may apply. Agreeing to texts isn't a condition of buying anything from us.

Reply STOP to any of our texts to stop getting them, or HELP for help. The full terms are on our text message terms page.

We don't share or sell mobile phone numbers with anyone else for their marketing. Your agreement to receive texts, and the record of when you gave it, is never shared with third parties. The company that sends our texts, listed below, handles your number only to deliver our messages.

Who helps us handle it: service providers that handle your information

A few companies process this information for us. Vercel hosts the site. Resend delivers the email that tells us about your request. GoHighLevel runs the chat on this site, sends our texts, and is where we keep track of requests and calls. Microsoft and Google provide the measurement tools listed above. Each handles the information under its own terms.

How long we keep it: how long we keep your information

We keep your request for as long as we're in touch, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices: your privacy choices

Send us a message and we'll show you what we hold, correct it or delete it. You can also block cookies in your browser, and the site will still work.

Analytics and Global Privacy Control

Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, and Microsoft Clarity measure visits and form submissions. They record that a form was sent, not what you wrote in it.

This site honors Global Privacy Control. If your browser sends that signal, we treat it as an opt-out of ad personalization and of sharing your information for advertising, and the ad storage and ad personalization settings are turned off for your visit. Analytics still runs.

Patient information

This site is for practice owners and their teams. Please don't send patient information through the form or the chat.

Changes: changes to this privacy policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
