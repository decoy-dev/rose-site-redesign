Text message terms

These terms cover the texts Rose Practice Solutions, LLC sends to people who ask for them in the chat on this site.

Last updated October 2026.

Who sends them: who sends Rose Practice Solutions text messages

Rose Practice Solutions, LLC, an orthodontic and dental marketing company. Our texts are sent through GoHighLevel.

How you sign up: signing up for text messages

You give us your mobile number in the chat on this site and tick the box agreeing to texts. The box is never ticked for you. Use a number that's yours, or one you have permission to use.

Agreeing to texts isn't a condition of buying anything from us.

What we send: the text messages we send

  • Replies to the question you asked in the chat.
  • Messages to set up or confirm a call.
  • Follow-up about the request you sent.
  • Now and then, news and offers about our services.

Some texts may be sent by automated means.

How often: how often we send text messages

Message frequency varies. It depends on the conversation.

What it costs: the cost of our text messages

We don't charge for our texts. Message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply.

How to stop: how to stop text messages

Reply STOP to any of our texts. We'll confirm by text, and we won't text you again unless you sign up again. You can also send us a message and ask us to stop.

Getting help: help with our text messages

Reply HELP to any of our texts, or send us a message.

Carriers: mobile carriers and text messages

Mobile carriers aren't liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your privacy: privacy and our text messages

We don't share or sell mobile phone numbers with anyone else for their marketing. Your agreement to receive texts, and the record of when you gave it, is never shared with third parties. Our privacy policy says what we collect and who helps us handle it.

Changes: changes to these text message terms

If we change these terms, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
