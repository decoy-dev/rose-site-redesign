Questions to ask any orthodontic marketing agency.

Ask them of every agency you talk to, including us.

Who will we actually talk to?

The founders who do the work. One of our founders replies within one business day.

Who owns the website and the ad accounts?

You do. The website, the domain and the content are yours, and the Google and Meta ad accounts are in your practice's name.

What do you report on?

Consults booked, not clicks. Every month you get what we did, what it brought in, and what we're changing next.

What happens after the phone rings?

Your team answers, and each consult is traced back to the ad that brought it. With the patient follow-up add-on, a patient concierge calls and texts your leads under your practice's name.

How long is the contract?

90 days from launch, then month to month with 30 days' notice.

Do you work with dentists or only orthodontists?

Both. Most of our work is built around orthodontic practices, and dental practices are welcome. Either way we work with privately owned practices, usually one to three locations. There is a page on orthodontic marketing and one on dental marketing.

What is patient follow-up?

Patient follow-up is an add-on to the Core Marketing Plan: a patient concierge calls and texts your new leads and past leads, at calling hours set with your practice, and books the consult into your practice's own patient management software. Any client can ask about it, and our sales team will talk through whether it fits your practice. How follow-up works.

Do you use AI?

Yes, for production, the way a photographer uses Lightroom. The strategy, the words, the design and every conversation come from a named person. No AI on the phone or in your inbox pretending to be one. The rest is in our AI policy.
