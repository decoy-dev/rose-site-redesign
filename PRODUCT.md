# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Owners and office managers of privately owned orthodontic practices (dental practices welcome), usually one to three locations, in the United States. They are clinicians or practice operators, not marketers, and most have been burned by an agency that reported "leads" and went quiet. They arrive comparing agencies and want to know three things fast: what Rose does, whether it brings in real consults, and who they will actually be dealing with.

## Product Purpose

Rose Practice Solutions is a five-founder marketing agency: websites and local SEO plus Google and Meta ads (the Core Marketing Plan, from $2,000 a month by location), with a patient follow-up add-on that calls and texts leads and books consults. The homepage exists to make that offer and its difference obvious in seconds, then get an email. Success is a practice owner leaving the hero knowing "more consults, from named people I'll know" and submitting the form.

## Brand Personality

Warm, specific, tended. Upscale and calm like a well-made product, human like a signed note from people you've met. The brand idea is "every change, signed": work is attributed to the named person who did it. Tagline: "Tended, not templated." The rose motif (the bloom) is the mark. The interface should feel crafted and alive, not corporate and not cute-for-its-own-sake.

## Anti-references

- Generic dental-marketing agency sites: stock smiles, teeth close-ups, "Grow your practice!" hype, lead-count bragging.
- Templated SaaS landing pages: three equal icon cards, gradient blobs, eyebrow labels on every section.
- Garden or flower metaphors beyond the tagline; leaves and plant graphics.
- Generated patients or generated smiles (their own published AI policy forbids them).
- Faceless agency pages that never show who does the work.

## Design Principles

1. Show the work, signed. Proof comes from product-style views of real deliverables (pages, ads, reports, follow-up), each attributed to a role or person.
2. The value prop should be readable in one glance: more consults booked, by people you'll know, for a public price.
3. Words earn their place. Cut copy until each section has one idea; let visuals carry the rest.
4. Crafted, living detail. Motion and material should feel physical and considered (paper, ink, the bloom), never decorative noise.
5. Identity is preserved: her palette, Instrument Serif and Plus Jakarta Sans, rose only in italics.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Every text and background pair is stated against the palette (cream on forest 8.7:1, ink on cream 13.78:1, deep rose on cream 8.43:1, label sage on cream 5.88:1). Honour prefers-reduced-motion everywhere; any looping or auto-playing motion longer than 5 seconds has a visible pause control (the live site's "Pause animations" toggle). Nothing is hidden behind an animation: the no-script and reduced-motion states are complete pages.
