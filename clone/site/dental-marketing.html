Dental marketing questions

What does a dental marketing agency do?

It gets your practice found and chosen by people nearby. At Rose Practice Solutions that's the Core Marketing Plan: websites and SEO, plus Google and Meta ads management. Patient follow-up is an add-on any client can ask about.

How much does dental marketing cost?

The Core Marketing Plan is $2,000 a month for one or two locations, $2,500 for three and $3,000 for four or more. Patient follow-up is an add-on, from $500 a month, priced by lead count. Ad spend is separate, paid to Google and Meta. The numbers on the pricing page are the numbers on the agreement.

Is ad spend included in the price?

No. Ad spend goes to Google and Meta directly, from ad accounts in your name, so the history stays yours. We suggest a budget on the first conversation, based on your market and how many new patients you can take.

Do you work with general dentists?

Yes. Most of our work is built around orthodontic practices, and dental practices are welcome. Either way we work with privately owned practices, usually one to three locations. If you're an orthodontist, start with the orthodontic marketing page.

Who calls our new patient leads?

With the patient follow-up add-on, a patient concierge calls under your practice's name. No AI on the phone or in an inbox pretending to be a person. The rest is in our AI policy.

Can we ask about patient follow-up?

Yes. Any client can ask about it. Patient follow-up is an add-on, and our sales team will talk it through with you to see whether it fits your practice.

We're part of a dental group. Is this for us?

Probably not, if the group already has a marketing team in-house. We're set up for the practice where the owner makes the call and wants to know the people doing the work.

What else can we buy outside the plan?

Three projects, each priced for your practice: brand and logo work, video and content, and a website without the ads. Patient follow-up is a monthly add-on to the Core Marketing Plan. Any client can ask about it, and our sales team confirms the fit. Both are on the add-ons and projects page.

Reputation management and dental consulting are coming soon.

How is Rose Practice Solutions different from other dental marketing companies?

Across our team we have more than 60 years of combined dental and orthodontic experience, clinical and business side. The people in the proposal are the people who do the work, and we judge it by visits booked, not by how many leads came in. The work is built from your practice and not from a template, and every monthly report says what we're changing next.

Is there a long contract?

The first 90 days from launch are a commitment. After that it's month to month, with 30 days' notice. The monthly report keeps coming until the last day.

Do you guarantee a number of new patients?

No. We never promise a patient count. You get a monthly report that shows how many leads came in, how many we reached and how many booked a visit. We judge the work by visits booked, not by how many leads came in.

Who owns the website and the ad accounts?

You do. You own the site, the domain and the content, and the Google and Meta ad accounts are in your own name.
